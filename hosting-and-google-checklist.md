# Elite Harvest Global Launch Checklist

## Required From Client

- Final logo file.
- Full office address for website and Google Maps.
- Final product list with categories, descriptions, and real product photos.
- Preferred domain name confirmation, for example `eliteharvestglobal.com`.
- Official business email account for domain, hosting, Google Business Profile, and Search Console ownership.

## Hosting, Domain, SSL, And Email

- Buy the `.com` domain through the official company email.
- Buy hosting through Hostinger or another reliable provider using the same official company email.
- Upload all website files to `public_html` or the configured static site root.
- Enable SSL/HTTPS from the hosting panel.
- Create the business email ID, for example `info@eliteharvestglobal.com`.
- Share cPanel or hosting login access with the client after launch.

## Google Setup

- Create or claim Google Business Profile using the official company email.
- Add company name, category, address, phone numbers, website URL, business hours, and photos.
- Complete Google verification.
- Add the website to Google Search Console.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` after the domain is live.
- Request indexing for the home page and main pages.
